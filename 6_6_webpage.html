Pale Peak

The city of Pale Peak lies to the south and east of the Great Valley, sitting where the Broadflow and Sunswift rivers meet. It is the third largest city by population in the Valley. It is a Titan Peak city, built inside the mountainous shell of a long-deceased Titan. The city lies within that shell, built upwards in layers, in a shape reflecting that of a mountain within a mountain.

The City's Districts

  1. Platinum Precipice
  2. Red Range
  3. Brown Band
  4. Silver Stratum
  5. Blue Bottom
  6. Pale Pier

A central feature of the city is the Zenith Prism, an ancient arcane wonder which is installed at the peak of the outer mountain shell. It allows sunlight to reach the city within, and it refracts the light causing the unusual washes of color which differentiate each of the city's districts.

The Pale Peak River Authority

The city is independant from the major political powers that surround it. A major reason for this is its economic dominance in the freight and trade industries, controlling the entire Great Valley's river system. The Pale Pier is the largest series of harbors in the Valley and is the center of all freight by water. The geography of the Great Valley make river-based freighting an important necessity. The Pale Peak River Authority (PPRA) exercises its power liberally, and its company board serves as the de facto governing force of the city-state.
